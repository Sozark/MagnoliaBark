1. Advanced Wound Care

In-Clinic & Mobile Home Visits

Chronic and non-healing wounds require targeted, evidence-based intervention to prevent infection, hospitalization, and limb loss. We diagnose the underlying vascular, pressure, or metabolic causes of delayed healing and build weekly restorative protocols.

  • Diabetic foot ulcers & neuropathic ulcer offloading
  • Venous stasis & arterial insufficiency leg ulcers
  • Stage I–IV pressure injuries (bedsores)
  • Non-healing post-surgical & traumatic wounds
  • Conservative sharp debridement & biofilm management
  • Multi-layer compression therapy & cellular tissue products
Request Wound Care Evaluation

2. Adult & Senior Primary Care

Preventive & Chronic Disease Management

Consistent primary care is the foundation of long-term independence and tissue healing. Our Nurse Practitioner manages chronic medical conditions, preventive screenings, and complex medication regimens with unhurried personal attention.

  • Type 1 & Type 2 diabetes & A1C optimization
  • Hypertension, high cholesterol & cardiovascular care
  • Annual wellness exams & preventive health screenings
  • Comprehensive medication reconciliation & deprescribing
  • Nutritional & metabolic support for tissue repair
  • Care coordination with local specialists & labs
Schedule Primary Care Visit

3. Cognitive Health Management

Memory Care & Caregiver Partnership

Early recognition and structured management of memory changes help older adults remain safe, respected, and supported at home. We partner closely with patients and family caregivers to build realistic care roadmaps.

  • Structured cognitive & memory baseline screenings
  • Alzheimer’s disease & dementia care management
  • Review of medications affecting alertness or memory
  • Home safety, fall-prevention & daily routine planning
  • Caregiver education, stress support & resource navigation
  • Coordination with neurology & community support programs
Book Cognitive Health Consultation

4. Specialized Ostomy Care

Colostomy, Ileostomy & Urostomy Support

Living comfortably with an ostomy starts with a secure seal, healthy peristomal skin, and practical hands-on education. We provide compassionate troubleshooting for new and established ostomy patients.

  • Post-operative stoma assessment & appliance sizing
  • Leakage troubleshooting & convex barrier fitting
  • Peristomal skin irritation, candidiasis & breakdown treatment
  • Patient & family pouch-change confidence coaching
  • Ostomy supply guidance & prescription documentation
  • In-clinic or discreet home-based ostomy follow-ups
Schedule Ostomy Care Visit

5. Mobile Home-Based Care

Orleans, Jefferson, Plaquemines & St. Bernard Parishes

For patients recovering from surgery or living with limited mobility, traveling to an outpatient clinic can worsen pain and fatigue. Our mobile clinician team brings comprehensive medical visits directly to your residence.

  • In-home wound debridement, dressing & progress tracking
  • Mobile primary care & chronic disease follow-up visits
  • In-home cognitive & medication safety assessments
  • Discreet home ostomy fitting & caregiver training
  • Serving private homes & assisted living residences
  • Coverage across 4 Greater New Orleans parishes

Patient & Caregiver Questions

Frequently Asked Clinical Questions

Select any question below to expand answers regarding referrals, insurance, and home-based care coverage.

Do I need a physician referral to schedule wound care or primary care at Magnolia Bark Clinic?

Many insurance plans allow patients and families to schedule directly with our Nurse Practitioner without a referral. We also accept direct referrals from primary care doctors, surgeons, podiatrists, and hospital discharge planners.

Which parishes are covered by your Home-Based Care mobile visits?

Our mobile clinician team provides in-home visits for homebound patients across Orleans Parish, Jefferson Parish, Plaquemines Parish, and St. Bernard Parish. Call our Algiers office at (504) 875-6629 to confirm mobile availability in your neighborhood.

How does Magnolia Bark Clinic treat non-healing diabetic or venous leg ulcers?

We evaluate both the wound bed and the systemic factors preventing closure. Treatment combines gentle debridement, infection control, moisture balance, compression or offloading, and active management of blood glucose, circulation, and nutrition.

Can family caregivers attend Cognitive Health and Ostomy Care appointments?

Yes. We strongly encourage spouses, adult children, and home caregivers to participate in cognitive health care planning and hands-on ostomy appliance training so everyone feels supported at home.
