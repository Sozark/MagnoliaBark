Our Mission & Clinical Philosophy

Patient-Centered Healing Rooted in Algiers & Greater New Orleans

Magnolia Bark Clinic (Wound Consultants) was founded to close the gap between specialized wound and ostomy care and everyday primary and cognitive health—giving patients one trusted clinical partner.

Meet Your Provider

Leachel Cook McMillan, FNP

As a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner, Leachel Cook McMillan established Magnolia Bark Clinic at 4480 General De Gaulle Drive to bring hospital-grade wound and ostomy expertise into an accessible, unhurried neighborhood setting.

Throughout her clinical career across Louisiana, she observed a recurring problem: patients with chronic ulcers, surgical wounds, or new ostomies were sent to high-volume corporate clinics that focused narrowly on dressing changes while overlooking uncontrolled blood sugar, vascular disease, medication interactions, or early cognitive decline.

By uniting Advanced Wound Care, Primary Care, Cognitive Health Management, Ostomy Care, and Mobile Home-Based Visits, Magnolia Bark treats the person behind the diagnosis.

Core Clinical Commitments
